Our lab spent 3rd–5th, September at the **Sejong University Convention Center** for the **35th International KOGO Annual Conference**.  
Great sessions, good discussions with colleagues across the genomics community, and a chance for our members to present and get feedback on their work.

Thanks to KOGO for another well-run meeting — and to our team for making the trip a fun one.

📍 Sejong University Convention Center, Seoul
